QUERY_6_JOIN
================================================================================

SQL QUERY:
SELECT
                b.title,
                b.price_gbp,
                b.price_inr,
                b.rating,
                b.in_stock,
                c.category_name
            FROM books b
            JOIN categories c
                ON b.category_id = c.category_id
            ORDER BY
                b.rating DESC,
                b.title
            LIMIT 10
